New feature

Tsubomi V2.3.0 adds Entropy, a treasure enchantment that repairs gear with XP faster than Mending, and a rare Warden drop that carries it.

Highlights (4)

  • Entropy, levels I to III: repairs tools, weapons and armour with XP at 1.75, 2.25 and 2.75 times the speed of Mending (3.5, 4.5 and 5.5 durability per XP). Fractions are rounded by chance, so nothing is lost. It cannot share an item with Mending.
  • A Warden sometimes drops an Entropy book: 12% (3% more per Looting level, 21% at Looting III), as level I, II or III with a 55%, 33% and 12% weighting. You get a message, a sound and sculk particles.
  • The book is an ordinary Enchanted Book that works in the Anvil and the Grindstone. Books for all three levels are in the Tsubomi creative tab.
  • Light on the server: it uses the game's own XP repair, with no extra tick and no change to other mods.

Warnings (2)

  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer and Java 25. No other mod is needed; Cherry is optional.
  • Tsubomi cannot be used together with Alpha. If any Alpha mod is installed the game will not start; remove Alpha's jars first.

Bug fix

Tsubomi V2.2.1 removes a duplicate Nether Star recipe. Only the 3x3 recipe remains.

Highlights (2)

  • The duplicate shapeless Nether Star recipe, which used one Diamond and one Ouka Alloy Block, is removed. The 3x3 recipe (4 Diamonds and 2 Ouka Alloy Blocks) is now the only one.
  • The 3x3 recipe still appears only when OUKA is installed. Nothing else changes.

New feature

Tsubomi V2.2.0 adds 500 building blocks in 50 new material series of 10 shapes, the Emblem of the Ocean, and a recipe that crafts the Nether Star.

Highlights (5)

  • 500 new blocks in 50 material series of 10 shapes each: bricks, chiseled, pillar, tiles, lamp, grate, reinforced plating, stairs, slab and wall. The series come in five themes: Japanese architecture, Sengoku metals, cyberpunk facilities, celestial crystals and sacred stones.
  • Every series is crafted from two plain materials in a 2x2 pattern and cut from its bricks at the stonecutter. Timber series are mined with an axe, the rest with a pickaxe.
  • Emblem of the Ocean: a new relic that cannot be crafted and is found only in Ocean Monuments. Elder Guardians drop it (15%, more with Looting), Guardians rarely (1.5%), and digging up Wet Sponges or Sea Lanterns underwater sometimes uncovers one (2%).
  • Craftable Nether Star: the Emblem of the Ocean, a Silence Armor Trim template, a Netherite Ingot, four Diamonds and two Ouka Alloy Blocks, or the five different items together. The recipes appear only when OUKA is installed.
  • Light on the server: no block has a block entity or a ticker, and the new blocks take no random ticks.

Warnings (2)

  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer and Java 25. No other mod is needed; Cherry is optional.
  • Tsubomi cannot be used together with Alpha. If any Alpha mod is installed the game will not start; remove Alpha's jars first.

New feature

Halia V1.3 adds the Vigil, a set of devices for guarding what you build: warded blocks only their owner can break, cameras, code locks, pass readers, snares and turrets. It also adds 46 doors, hatches and gates that open with real motion, and the Doorwright's Bench, where you design your own door.

Highlights (6)

  • The Vigil: 593 warded versions of building blocks, broken only by their owner; Watch Eyes you can look through from a Watch Slate; Code Plates, Coded Doors, Coded Chests and other coded containers; Watch Passes and Pass Readers; Tripbeams, Stir Lamps and the Klaxon; 40 snares disguised as ordinary blocks; and the Watch Turret, which shoots hostile mobs that come into range.
  • 46 doors, hatches and gates with real motion: Cell Bars, glass sliders, the Clean-room Door, the Foundry Door, the Paper Screen Door, 16 Drapes, six Floor and Slide Hatches, the Tavern Doors and the Bulkhead Door.
  • Big doors: eight 3 x 3 Grand Gates, the 3 x 2 Iris Door, the Roller Shutter (a stack of panels that rolls up into the ceiling), the 2 x 2 Corroded Hatch with its ladder, and the Barrier Veil, a wall of light you raise between two corners with the Veil Emitter.
  • The Coach-house Door and the Keep Door: 4 x 5 double doors whose frame is made of the block you craft them with (one door and five or more of one block).
  • The Mimic Fence Gate takes on the look of the blocks on either side of it.
  • The Doorwright's Bench: choose one of 18 movements, the opening time, auto-close, opening when a player comes near, the redstone rule, the double-door pairing, one of 12 sounds and a code of up to six digits, then make a Crafted Door from any frame block and two panel blocks (fire, a portal, water and lava too). Crafted doors can be re-set at the bench later.

Warnings (3)

  • Requirements: Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer, Java 25. For multiplayer, Halia must also be installed on the server.
  • Fixed: since V1.2, breaking an Aerie storage block dropped it without the link to what it stored, so a moved block came back empty. It now keeps its contents. Contents already lost this way cannot be restored.
  • Worlds from V1.2 open as they are. Do not go back to 1.2 with a world that holds Vigil devices or the new doors: they would be deleted.

New feature

Limitless is now called Ouka JJK. The OUKA Beacon is rebalanced: its blessings are a quarter as strong, its recipe needs two Heavy Cores, and it now wears out with use and is repaired with a Heavy Core.

Highlights (4)

  • Limitless is renamed Ouka JJK: the creative tab, the Controls category and the technique wheel use the new name. The techniques and items keep their names.
  • The OUKA Beacon's blessings are a quarter as strong as before; the range is unchanged. The terminal shows the new strength. Resistance no longer makes you immune, and fire immunity and full night vision need a higher level.
  • The OUKA Beacon's recipe needs two Heavy Cores, in the bottom corners.
  • The OUKA Beacon has durability. It wears faster the more players it blesses and the higher its levels are set, and stops when it breaks. Right-click it with a Heavy Core to repair it to full. The terminal shows how much is left, and a beacon keeps its durability when you pick it up.

Warnings (2)

  • With the graphics option Improved Transparency turned on (it is off by default), the Hollow Purple sphere is still drawn in front of water. This will be fixed in 1.7.2.
  • Before you install 1.7.1, remove the old OUKA jar (1.7.0 or older) from the mods folder and keep only one copy of GeckoLib 5.5.6. It needs Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, and Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer. Install it on both the client and the server.

New feature

Halia V1.2 adds the Aerie, a storage network you build from an Aerie Heart, conduits and cells: every item and fluid you own, searchable from one console, moved in and out by Talon Intakes and Outlets and crafted on demand by Artificers. The Halia Blade gains 20 named blades, 7 slash arts, and souls that a blade collects and spends.

Highlights (8)

  • The Aerie: place an Aerie Heart, join devices with Aerie Conduits, put Clutch Cells in a Clutch Bay, and open a Roost Console to see and search everything stored. 37 kinds of device, most of them in 16 colours.
  • Moving and crafting: Talon Intakes and Outlets move items and fluids between the Aerie and any chest or machine; Artificers craft on demand from patterns written at a Scribe Console; Updraft Masts and Field Consoles reach the Aerie without wires.

  • 20 new named blades: the Gale Fang and Tempest Fang lines, Ironcrag, Ashen Crag, six Plumes from wood to netherite, the Weathered Blade, Evenfall, Heartwood and the Raptors. Most are forged on the anvil from a material box; some drop from bosses; zombies sometimes carry a Weathered Blade; and a nameless blade on a stand caught in a Wither's blast becomes the Ashen Crag.
  • 7 new slash arts: Blossom End, Void Slash, Circle Slash, Vertical and Horizontal Drive, Wave Edge and Piercing. Drives send a flying slash ahead of you.
  • Souls: a blade held while you defeat enemies gathers souls from their experience. Summoned swords now cost souls instead of experience, and refining a blade on the anvil gives it souls.
  • Durability and refining: each named blade now has its own durability, and refining raises a blade's damage and its maximum durability (up to refine 200).
  • Stellar Remote SS-05 Tesseract: a fifth attack, with its own film. A cube of light 640 blocks on a side rises round your crosshair; its ground is cut into cells that are lifted, turned and hung in the air ring by ring from the middle outward, and a cube of light locks in the sky.

  • Changes to V1.1 blades: a blade's hit is 1 lower than before at the same base value; newly made named blades have lower base attack and durability than in V1.1 (for example Yamato: attack 9 → 7, durability 100 → 40); summoned swords need souls. Blades already in your world keep the durability they were made with.

  • Worlds from V1.1 open as they are. Do not go back to 1.1 with a world that holds Aerie devices or the new blades: they would be deleted.
  • The Stellar Remote destroys very large areas permanently, and SS-05 reshapes a 640-block area at once — back up your world. Anyone standing inside the cube when its wave passes dies.

New feature

Cherry's base-defence and transit update: villages keep guard villagers, a security terminal and robot protect your own base, an automatic turret fires whichever Cherry gun you mount in it, and a glass elevator, a heavy cargo lift and an escalator carry you, your cargo and your vehicles. The robot, the terminal, the turret and both lifts run on power: a redstone signal, or, with OUKA V1.2 or later installed, OUKA's grid.

Highlights (9)

  • Guard villagers: the first time a player comes near a village that has a bell, a bed and a villager, it is given 2 guards (3 with ten or more beds, 4 with twenty or more). They carry a halberd, a crossbow and a shield, fight hostile mobs within 16 blocks, back away from creepers and shoot them from a distance, and one replacement arrives every 5 minutes while the village is below its quota.
  • Guards turn on a player only after that player has hurt a villager or a guard, and let go 2 minutes after the last offence. They never fight each other, villagers or golems, and they never turn on a player in Peaceful.
  • The Security Terminal and the Security Robot: whoever places a terminal is its administrator, and right-clicking it enrols the nearest player within 2.5 blocks. A robot linked to a terminal patrols within 12 blocks of its post and, at any unregistered player it sees within 16 blocks, sounds a siren, warns for 2 seconds and then fires its stun rifle every 1.5 seconds: 2 damage and a 3-second stun. Registered players, guards, and players in Creative or Spectator are never stunned.
  • The robot's stun rifle runs on a 400 kJ battery that holds 20 shots. The robot recharges within 16 blocks of its terminal while the terminal holds energy, and the terminal fills from a redstone signal beside it. An empty robot still sounds the siren and aims, but holds fire until it is charged. With OUKA V1.2 or later installed, a robot or terminal within an OUKA pylon's reach also charges from OUKA's grid.
  • The Automatic Turret mounts on a ceiling, a wall or a floor and turns through 360 degrees. Put one Cherry gun in it and feed it that gun's magazines (a hopper works): it then tracks hostile mobs, and angered neutral mobs, within 32 blocks or the gun's range, whichever is shorter, and fires at that gun's own rate with its own sound. It never shoots players, villagers, guards, robots, golems or pets. Only its owner and operators can open or break it, and a broken turret keeps its gun, its reserve and its aim.
  • Power for the turret, the glass elevator, the cargo lift, the terminal and the robot comes from a redstone signal beside the device (beside the controller, for a lift), and that is all a game without OUKA needs. With OUKA V1.2 or later installed, a device within an OUKA pylon's reach (64 blocks) otherwise draws from OUKA's grid, including its SMES batteries. A device without power says what it is waiting for.
  • The Aero-Glass High-Speed Elevator: a 3x3 glass cabin on a controller and a maglev rail column, in a shaft you build up to 128 blocks tall. It cruises at up to 16 blocks a second with a smooth start and stop, rings a chime on arrival and opens its doors a moment later. Pick a floor on the holographic panel in the cabin or at the call button on a landing; the doors reopen if someone stands in them, and a landing door never opens onto an empty shaft. Closed, the cabin keeps the air in on the Moon and the cold out on Mars.
  • The Titan-Hydraulic Heavy Cargo Lift: a square platform from 3x3 to 7x7 blocks on scissor legs over a pit you frame, rising up to 24 blocks at 2 blocks a second. Yellow-black gates close around it and an amber beacon spins while it moves. New feature

Fixes a bug that could leave your Minecraft unable to start. New feature

Corvus can now update itself. If anything goes wrong the previous version is put back automatically: the new build has to actually start before the old one is discarded. Two honest limitations.
