  2. Shipped · OUKA

    V1.7.0 · 2026-10-18

    Limitless 1.7.0 — Infinity, the domains and the King of Curses

    OUKA brings in a complete set of sorcery: play as Gojo with Infinity, Lapse Blue, Reversal Red, Hollow Purple and the Infinite Void domain, or as Sukuna with Dismantle, Cleave, Open and the Malevolent Shrine. The weapons Kamutoke and Hiten, the Blindfold and the Character Guide come with it. It adds 10 items and 1 block, and carries everything from 1.6.2.2.

    Open →

  3. Shipped · Tsubomi

    V2.0.0 · 2026-10-18

    Tsubomi V2 — 200 new blocks, the Enchanted Apple, a craftable spawner

    Tsubomi V2.0.0 adds 200 building blocks in 20 material series of 10 shapes, the Enchanted Apple, a craftable Monster Spawner and a recipe for every Spawn Egg.

    Open →

  4. Shipped · Halia

    V1.2.1 · 2026-10-16

    Halia V1.2.1 — fix for a crash when opening the inventory

    Halia V1.2.1 fixes a crash: with OUKA also installed, opening the inventory in creative mode closed the game.

    Open →

  5. Shipped · ASTRAEA

    V1.5.4 · 2026-10-14

    Defence

    All 25 things of Defence are rebuilt at the size of the real thing: a missile silo eight blocks tall with its missile standing ready, an interceptor battery on its turntable, devices that move and light up as their state changes, and armour, goggles and a mask with bodies of their own when worn. OUKA 1.4.0 or newer is required, as before.

    Open →

  6. Shipped · Cherry

    V1.2.1 · 2026-10-13

    Guards, robots, turrets and transit

    Cherry's base-defence and transit update: villages keep guard villagers, a security terminal and robot protect your own base, an automatic turret fires whichever Cherry gun you mount in it, and a glass elevator, a heavy cargo lift and an escalator carry you, your cargo and your vehicles. The robot, the terminal, the turret and both lifts run on power: a redstone signal, or, with OUKA V1.2 or later installed, OUKA's grid.

    Open →

  7. Shipped · Alpha

    v4.4.0.2 · 2026-09-28

    Alpha is retired: this update switches it off

    <b>This is the last Alpha release. It switches Alpha off.</b> The 13 mods are replaced by placeholders that add nothing to the game, and nothing new has been added. Blocks that Alpha added turn into empty air, and once a world has been saved that way they do not come back. <b>Back up your worlds before you update.</b>

    Open →

  8. Shipped · Aureum

    1.0.0 · 2026-08-30

    First release — memory savings that stay out of chunk rendering

    A separate optimisation mod for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric), apart from Alpha. It works on game logic, memory and the server tick, never touches chunk rendering, and so runs beside Sodium and the Alpha pack. For higher FPS, install Sodium.

    Open →
