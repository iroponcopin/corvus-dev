4.4.0.2

Alpha

Alpha has officially ended distribution. It was a pack of 13 modules — firearms, rail, vehicles, building, disasters and other dimensions — and no further version will be released. Its last release, 4.4.0.2, switches it off.

Retirement updateChangelog

What has ended

Distribution of Alpha ended on 2026-09-28. 4.4.0.2 is the last release: it replaces the 13 mods with placeholders that add nothing to the game.

What has been withdrawn

Every earlier download, Alpha's recipes, and its install guide, gates, features, roadmap and known-issues pages are gone from this site. The changelog keeps only this notice.

Your worlds

Back up your worlds before you update. Blocks that Alpha added turn into empty air, and once a world has been saved that way they do not come back. What you built above Y=319 with ordinary blocks is kept.

What continues

OUKA, Cherry and Aureum are separate and are not affected. Corvus keeps updating them.
