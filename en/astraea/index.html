Service ended

ASTRAEA's service has ended

ASTRAEA ended its service on 16 October 2026. Thank you to everyone who played it, from V1.0 to the final release, V1.5.4 "Defence".

Thank you, too, to everyone who told us about its bugs. It meant a great deal to see the blast doors and locks, the celestial blade and tools, and machines the size of buildings standing in your worlds.

What happens now

  1. In Corvus Store, ASTRAEA is archived and can no longer be installed.
  2. Corvus Agent disables and removes ASTRAEA from servers automatically, after taking a world backup, on its first sync on or after 23 October 2026.
  3. Its download is no longer offered on this site. The pages stay up as a record of ASTRAEA.

Please read this

When ASTRAEA is removed, its blocks and items disappear from your worlds. Back up any world you want to keep before 23 October 2026.

OUKA is not affected.

Thank you, all of you, for everything.
